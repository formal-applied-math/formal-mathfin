## Corrections to the earlier survey

- **Rough Heston was incorrectly marked closed.** Prömel–Scheffels require
  diffusion Hölder exponent `ξ > 1/[2(1−α)]` for kernel `(t−s)^−α`. The
  square-root exponent `ξ = 1/2` does not meet this when `α > 0`. Reading their
  Remark 2.2 without the full Assumption 2.1 caused the earlier overstatement.
- **Stefan uniqueness is now a narrower question.** The September 2026 result
  gives global uniqueness if the minimal physical solution is continuous on
  some `(0, ε)`. A distinct theorem about maximal weak solutions cannot be
  substituted for uniqueness among physical solutions.
- **The equilibrium-control paper proves a positive result.** Its introductory
  description of the earlier open problem is not its conclusion. The issue
  targets the explicitly deferred action-dependent diffusion extension and
  requires all additional hypotheses to be stated.
- **Static Gaussian Kyle uniqueness has a reported resolution.** Monteiro–Tourky,
  ANU Working Paper 708 (August 2026), claim uniqueness for Borel strategies
  with pointwise optimality. MF-OP-008 records that claim without asserting an
  independent proof check. The continuous-time literature also needs a precise
  account of equilibrium classes and information assumptions before a new
  question is filed.
- **Nearby entropy results do not settle the two selected duality questions.**
  The October 2026 global SPX/VIX calibration work concerns feasibility and
  dependence; ordinary MOT dual attainment has a different objective and
  constraints. Pennanen–Perkkiö's semistatic application uses finitely many
  static derivatives, rather than a whole terminal marginal law.
