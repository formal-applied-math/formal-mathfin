# Research-problem catalogue audit — 2026-11-19 (round 6)

Round 6 re-checked every lead the [older survey](../open-problems.md) still carried: ten Tier 1
entries that never became records, the 61-item Tier 3 pool (D1–D61), the four areas the coverage
map listed as never swept (implied-volatility market models, large-markets FTAP, high-frequency
econometrics, real options), and a hunt for elementary conjectures, the kind of problem the two
solved records (MF-OP-006, MF-OP-007) were. Seven research passes searched first for resolutions
and only then for openness evidence. Their candidates then went through an adversarial pass that
looked for later papers, mainly through papers citing each source. The working records, with
every search, source and verdict, are in [candidates-2026-11-19.json](candidates-2026-11-19.json).

## Statements in Lean

Each record now says whether it can be stated against the library (`formalization.lean_statement`).
Statability decides nothing about whether a problem is catalogued. A problem that needs objects the
library lacks keeps them in `needs`, as frontier `def:` nodes with issues (#532–#536, #538–#544),
and `python3 -m tools.verify.research_problems needs` ranks them by the problems they block.
Three questions are stated today, closed by `sorry`, in `MathFinFrontier/OpenProblems/`:
MF-OP-008 (Kyle uniqueness), MF-OP-016 and MF-OP-017 (complete mixability). Two of them are
reported resolutions, MF-OP-008 and MF-OP-017. For those, a kernel-checked proof of the stated
theorem moves the record to `solved` without anyone having to referee the paper.

## Corrections to the survey

The pass found these survey descriptions wrong or out of date. Each correction rests on the source
named in the candidate file. Several were read only as abstracts, which the file records.

- **D20.** "Ergodicity needs proportional cancellation, undecidable-adjacent" is unsupported:
  Huang–Rosenbaum prove ergodicity under general assumptions.
- **D27.** The framing "beyond completely monotone kernels" is misdirected, since power-law
  kernels are completely monotone.
- **D28.** The DPP holds on the lifted Wasserstein state (Pham–Wei). Only time-inconsistent
  problems break it.
- **D35.** The general-semimartingale duality is proved for càdlàg obstacles (Rodrigues 2025),
  and for irregular obstacles over all equivalent local martingale measures (arXiv 2611.05116).
- **D40.** Regularisation is proved necessary under the usual conditions. Only the raw-filtration
  variant is open.
- **D42.** "Not yet addressed" has been outdated since Calvia–Ferrari (2021).
- **D51, D58, D60, D61.** These are closed in the senses recorded in the candidate file.
- **§3.** The "elusive" quote is from 2011/2013, and the question was answered by a counterexample.
- **§4.** "Every positive result is semilinear" is false (Grohs–Herrmann 2021).
- **§5 and §12.** These now carry reported resolutions (Lee, September 2026; Bayraktar–Lawless and
  Tai, 2026).
